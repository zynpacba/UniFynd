OUR STORY

About UniFynd

UniFynd is a campus-focused lost-and-found platform designed to help students, faculty, and university staff reconnect with their belongings in one organized place.

The idea behind UniFynd started with a simple problem: lost and found notices are often scattered across group chats, social media, and campus offices, making it difficult to find what we are looking for.

We aim to change that by bringing campus communities together through a smarter, simpler, and safer lost-and-found experience.

Our Mission

To make finding and returning lost belongings simpler, safer, and more efficient.

Our Vision

To build a connected campus community where lost belongings can find their way back home.

SIMPLE STEPS

How UniFynd Works

From reporting an item to getting it back. Four simple steps to reconnect with your belongings.

  1. 1

    Report an Item

    Sign in and create a lost or found listing with a description, category, and campus location.

  2. 2

    Find a Match

    Browse available listings or discover possible matches suggested by our AI-powered matching system.

  3. 3

    Send a Request

    Found a possible match? Send a contact or ownership request and wait for the listing owner's approval.

  4. 4

    Reconnect

    Once your request is approved, communicate privately, verify ownership, and arrange a safe return.

✓

You can browse listings without an account. AI matches are suggestions, not proof of ownership.
