Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers, get support, and make the most of UniFynd.

Do I need an account to browse items?

No. You can browse lost and found items freely. You only need an account to post items, manage your listings, or communicate with other users.

How do I report a lost or found item?

Log in to your UniFynd account and select Report Item. Choose whether you lost or found an item, enter its description, category, university, campus and location, and submit your listing.

Report an Item →
How does AI matching work?

UniFynd uses AI-powered semantic matching to compare lost and found item descriptions, even when different words are used to describe similar belongings.

The system suggests possible matches based on item information and campus details. A suggested match does not guarantee that two listings refer to the same item. Users must verify the match themselves.

How do I prove an item belongs to me?

Open the relevant item listing and submit an ownership claim. Provide identifying details that can help the person who found the item verify your ownership.

These details may include unique marks, accessories, or other characteristics that are not publicly displayed in the listing.

Is my contact information visible to other users?

Your personal contact information is not publicly displayed on UniFynd listings.

Users must send a contact or ownership claim request. Messaging becomes available only after the listing owner approves the request.

What do the listing statuses mean?

Listing statuses help you understand the current progress of a lost or found item.

Active The item has not yet been returned.
Returned The item has been reunited with its owner.
What should I do if I find an item on campus?

Create a found item listing on UniFynd and include where and when you found the item.

Keep unique identifying details private to help verify ownership. When appropriate, hand the item over to campus security or the university's lost-and-found office.
